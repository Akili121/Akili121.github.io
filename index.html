Bio

My research develops data-driven and AI-enabled methods to improve the resilience of transportation infrastructure under natural disasters. I study how floods affect road networks and how post-disaster restoration decisions can better account for accessibility, social vulnerability, and equity. My current work uses graph neural networks and network analysis to assess disaster impacts and support resource-constrained restoration planning.

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, specializing in Engineering Management, at the University of Alberta. I am supervised by Dr. Xinming Li from the University of Alberta and co-supervised by Dr. Lingzi Wu from the University of Washington. I was a visiting PhD student at the University of Washington and previously a visiting master's student at the National University of Singapore. I received my M.Sc. in Engineering Management from Central South University and my B.Eng. in Engineering Management from Central South University of Forestry and Technology.
